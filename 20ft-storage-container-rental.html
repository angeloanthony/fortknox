A 20ft container comfortably holds the contents of a 1–2 bedroom apartment: a queen bed, dresser, couch, dining table and chairs, washer/dryer, refrigerator, TV, and 30–40 boxes. It also works well for tools, ATVs, motorcycles, small business inventory, and contractor supplies.
