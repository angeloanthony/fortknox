Our Story

At Fort Knox Self Storage, we understand that finding the right storage solution is about more than just space—it's about peace of mind. Located in Vernal, Utah, we've built our reputation on providing secure, clean, and accessible storage facilities that our community can trust.

The Fort Knox Promise: We provide high-security, mouse-proof, and fireproof storage at competitive prices. Whether you are buying a 40ft steel container or renting a 20ft unit, we offer personalized local service and 24/7 yard access with a quick call to James at 435-828-7710.
