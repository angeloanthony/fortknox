What are the exact dimensions of a 40ft storage container?

A standard 40-foot steel shipping container is 40 ft long × 8 ft wide × 8.5 ft tall. That provides approximately 320 square feet of floor space and about 2,390 cubic feet of volume. The High Cube variant is 9.5 ft tall for an extra foot of vertical clearance. Double doors are 7.7 ft wide × 7.5 ft tall.

What fits in a 40ft storage container?

A 40ft container comfortably holds the entire contents of a 3–4 bedroom home plus a full garage — all furniture, appliances, boxes, an ATV or motorcycle, and tools. It's also the go-to choice for ranchers, oilfield workers, and contractors in the Uintah Basin who need large, secure storage.

What is the difference between the 40ft Standard and 40ft High Cube?

The Standard is 8.5 feet tall (interior ~7.9 ft). The High Cube is 9.5 feet tall (interior ~8.9 ft) — one full extra foot of clearance. The High Cube is preferred for tall items like lumber, shelving systems, or workshop use. Call James at 435-828-7710 to ask which variant is available.

Is the 40ft container mouse-proof?

Yes. Fort Knox containers are all-steel sealed shipping containers — no wood framing, no gaps, no entry points for mice, rodents, or insects. Traditional stick-built storage units in Vernal use wood framing that rodents can chew through. Steel containers eliminate that problem entirely.
