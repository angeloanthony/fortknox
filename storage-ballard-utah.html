Ballard is a small community north of Vernal along US-40 in the Uintah Basin. While there are no dedicated storage facilities in Ballard itself, Fort Knox Self Storage is located just a short drive south at 2225 South 3250 East in Vernal — making it the closest secure storage option for Ballard residents and families.

Why Ballard Residents Choose Fort Knox

Ballard and the surrounding rural areas of the Basin have a particular problem with mice — especially during fall and winter when rodents move indoors. If you've ever found mouse droppings in your garage, shed, or trailer, you know the damage they can do to furniture, clothing, wiring, and equipment.

Fort Knox is the only storage facility in the area built entirely from steel shipping containers. No wood framing, no gaps, no rodent entry points. Your belongings stay untouched whether you store them for a month or a year.

Easy Access From Ballard

Take US-40 south through Vernal, then turn east on 2225 South. The facility is at 3250 East with a clearly marked entrance. The drive from Ballard takes roughly 15 minutes depending on where you are. The driveways are wide enough for any truck-and-trailer combination, and 24/7 access means you're never restricted by office hours.

Serving the Entire Basin

Fort Knox serves residents in Ballard, Vernal, Naples, Jensen, Maeser, LaPoint, Roosevelt, and the broader Uintah Basin. Whether you need a 20ft unit for apartment contents or a 40ft container for a full household, James has availability and can get you moved in the same day.
