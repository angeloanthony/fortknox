New or Used — Choose Your Condition

🆕

Brand New (One-Trip)

Factory fresh, used only once for shipping. Pristine condition with no dents, rust, or wear. Best for premium storage or living conversions.

Best for: Premium storage, container homes, business use
💎

Cargo-Worthy (CW)

Certified cargo-worthy — 100% wind and water tight. Excellent condition with minimal cosmetic wear. Best value for the money.

Best for: Residential storage, workshops, job sites — most popular choice
👌

Wind & Water Tight (WWT)

Fully weather-tight with normal cosmetic wear — minor dents or surface rust that doesn't affect function. Solid budget option.

Best for: Budget buyers, equipment storage, farms and ranches
🔧

As-Is

Functional but may need some attention. Great for DIY projects, modifications, or non-critical storage. Lowest prices available.

Best for: DIY projects, hobbyists, temporary or secondary storage
